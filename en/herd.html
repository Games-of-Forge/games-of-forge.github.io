Herd, the first musical deckbuilder.

A turn-based tactical card game where every card played reshapes the music of the duel. The track you are hearing is the match you are playing.

Unreal Engine 5 · PC / Steam · 1 versus 1 · Early Access coming

Play the demo, free Steam page

The game

Playing a card means writing a bar

Two players face off turn by turn. The cards they play feed a composition built live: the tactical choices and the music are the same thing. It is the mechanic that took the most research, and it is the one that makes the game.

What the studio did

Game direction, systems and musical mechanic design, Unreal Engine 5 development in C++, team and budget management, and getting it onto Steam. The project started as a commission from an international client, and Games of Forge is credited as its developer and publisher.

Why it matters

A new idea that actually holds up in a player's hands: the demo is public, free, and the game is heading into Early Access. That full chain, from concept to storefront, is what we make available in co-development.
