The studio

From concept to players' hands.

Games of Forge grew out of directing Herd, a tactical card game commissioned by an international client. The studio designs, develops and publishes it: the demo has been free to play on Steam since June 2026, with Early Access to follow. That is our starting point: holding a product vision and getting it in front of real players.

What we do

Turn a rough intention into a game loop that holds up: systems design, fast prototyping, design direction, and the production work around it (budget, schedule, contractors).

The studio is led by Pierre Aceituno, a game director trained at ENJMIN who came up through production. See his track record.

How we are set up

A permanent creative direction, plus a network of regular collaborators (art direction, programming, sound design) brought in project by project.

We size the team to the actual need. You do not pay for a structure that is not working on your game.
