The mod

A sandbox tells you nothing on its own

TOR Fantasy Adventures is a sub-mod sitting on top of The Old Realms, the Warhammer overhaul for Bannerlord. The Old Realms brings the world: the races, the troops, the magic. TORFA adds what it was missing, a reason to move. You refound a clan, you swear a first oath, and from there every campaign hangs off a written arc.

The dwarf path, end to end

The published version ships one complete playable line: refounding the clan, the First Oath at Karak Hirn before King Alrik, a quest arc against the Urk bandits across the four valleys of Karak Angazhar, and a climax facing Grobzag Three-Teeth's WAAAGH. The other races (Empire, Bretonnia, Mousillon, Vampire Counts, Greenskins) are in progress.

Why I do it

Because it is the one place where I design and implement it myself, with no budget to hold and nobody to answer to. A mod meets real players within days, and they tell you very directly what does not work. It is the cheapest laboratory I know for testing a design intention.

Inside the mod

What I built into it

  • Battle A three-layer AI: the general sets a doctrine, regiments pick their stance, soldiers deal with the ground
  • Regiments Units that are genuinely yours: named captains, specialisation paths, a progress tree, equipment
  • War camp A mobile hub that follows the army, with fatigue tuned culture by culture
  • Mandates Anti-bandit contracts that rot if you neglect them, across five escalation levels up to a standing army
  • Racial resources Each people has its own, oath gold for the dwarfs
  • Staging Intro, coronation and ceremony cinematics, an ambient playlist per arc, an after-battle report regiment by regiment
