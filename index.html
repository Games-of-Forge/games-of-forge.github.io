The studio

Born from a commission, carried to Steam.

Games of Forge grew around Herd. An international client wanted a musical game. We had to invent the mechanic that went with it, make it hold up in multiplayer, then put it in the hands of real players. The demo shipped on Steam in June 2026, Early Access follows. That is the road we bring back to the table when we work on someone else's game.

What we do

We start from an intention, usually a vague one, and pull a game loop out of it. Systems design, prototyping, design direction. And the production behind it: budget, schedule, contractors, and the calls that get a project shipped instead of stretched.

How we work

A permanent creative direction, plus regular collaborators in art, code and sound, brought in project by project. We build the team to the size of the need. Nobody bills you for time they do not spend on your game.

Client work, in production

Braket, a live tournament platform

A game is one kind of delivery. Braket is another: a white-label tournament platform for Steam games, which we build and maintain for its operator, Slyseed. Studios and organisers open their own arena, run their tournaments, and reward their winners inside Steam. It runs in production today, and it is not our IP. We are the team that builds it.

The product

One arena per game

Single and double elimination, round robin, swiss, open lobbies, from one-on-one to team formats. Each customer gets their own address, their own branding, their own data.

The build

Web platform and game SDK

TypeScript and Fastify on the server, Steam sign-in and Steam rewards, plus an Unreal and Unity SDK so a game can report its own match results.
