The studio

From concept to players' hands.

Games of Forge grew out of directing Herd, a tactical card game commissioned by an international client. The studio designs, develops and publishes it: the demo has been free to play on Steam since June 2026, with Early Access to follow. That is our starting point, and the reason we can promise a result rather than a slide deck.

What we do

Turn a rough intention into a game loop that holds up: systems design, fast prototyping, design direction, and the production work around it. Budget, schedule, contractors, and the hard calls that keep a project shippable.

How we are set up

A permanent creative direction, plus a network of regular collaborators in art direction, programming and sound design, brought in project by project. We size the team to the actual need: you never pay for a structure that is not working on your game.
