Profile

Building with a team, and deciding when the project needs it

PA

I started on the production side, on teams of up to 25 people split across two studios, before moving into game direction. That double background serves me daily: I hold a creative intention without losing sight of the budget, the schedule, and the people doing the work.

On Herd I was producer on the first version, from 2021 to 2022, then game director. The brief called for a musical game: we spent a long time looking for the right mechanic, the one where music does not illustrate the match but constitutes it. Then it had to hold up in multiplayer, hold a budget, and ship a demo.

I like building with people and taking care of them. It does not stop me from making the call when the project depends on it.
