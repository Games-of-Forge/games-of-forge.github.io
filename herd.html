Herd, the first musical deckbuilder.

A turn-based tactical card game where every card played reshapes the music of the duel. The track you are hearing is the match you are playing.

Unreal Engine 5 · PC / Steam · 1 versus 1 · Early Access coming

Play the demo, free Steam page

The game

Playing a card means writing a bar

Two players, one turn each. The cards they play feed a composition built live, and the Mélodus listening to it switch sides. Your tactical choices and the music are the same thing. That is what took us the most research, and it is what makes the game.

What the studio did

All of it: game direction, systems, the musical mechanic, Unreal Engine 5 development in C++, the team, the budget, and getting it onto the store. The project started as a commission from an international client. On Steam, Games of Forge is credited as developer and publisher.

What it proves

A new idea can hold up in a player's hands. The demo is public, free, and the game is heading into Early Access. Between the first intention and the store page there was everything else, and that everything else is what we make available in co-development.
